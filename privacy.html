Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HamaraVideo, operated by Sribharat GPS Tracker Private Limited ("we", "us"), collects and uses your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, mobile number, email address.
  • Content you provide: video descriptions, shop details, offer text, and any images you upload for video generation.
  • Payment data: payment status and reference IDs from Razorpay. We never receive or store your card numbers, UPI PIN, or banking passwords.
  • Usage data: generation history, device/browser information, and log data for security and troubleshooting.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate your videos — your descriptions are sent to third-party AI providers solely to produce the video you requested.
  • To manage your account, credits, and payment history.
  • To notify you when your video is ready (SMS, email, or WhatsApp).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse of the Service.
  • To send occasional offers, which you can opt out of at any time.

3. Sharing of information

  • AI providers: your video description is shared with our AI generation partners only to create your video.
  • Razorpay: processes payments as an independent payment aggregator under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting: data is stored on secure servers of our hosting provider.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or lawful government request.

4. Data retention

Account and transaction records are retained as required by Indian tax and accounting laws. Generated videos and their descriptions are retained in your account history so you can re-download them; you may request deletion of your account and history at any time.

5. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, access controls, and secure credential storage. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data as required under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable Indian data protection law.

6. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent for promotional communication.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.

Write to us at the contact below for any of these requests.

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies/local storage for login sessions and basic analytics. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

8. Children

The Service is intended for users aged 18 and above.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
