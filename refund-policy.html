Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

HamaraVideo is a digital service that generates AI-powered promotional videos. Payments on HamaraVideo are made to purchase video generation credits. This policy explains when cancellations and refunds are possible.

1. Nature of the service

Credits purchased on HamaraVideo are digital goods. Each video generation consumes credits and incurs real processing costs at the moment of generation. Because of this, a credit that has already been used to generate a video cannot be refunded.

2. Failed or faulty generations

  • If a video generation fails due to a technical error on our side, the credit used is automatically returned to your account. No request is needed.
  • If the credit is not returned automatically, contact us within 48 hours and we will restore it after verification.

3. Refund of unused credits

  • Unused credits may be refunded if you request a refund within 7 days of purchase.
  • Refunds are calculated only on the unused portion of the purchased pack.
  • Free or bonus credits carry no monetary value and are not refundable.

4. Cancellation of a purchase

An order can be cancelled only before any credit from that order has been used. Once even one credit from an order is used for generation, the order is considered consumed in part and only the unused portion is eligible under Section 3.

5. How refunds are processed

  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method (UPI / card / net banking) via our payment partner Razorpay.
  • Refunds are typically credited within 5–7 business days after approval, subject to your bank's processing time.

6. What is not covered

  • Dissatisfaction with the creative style of a successfully generated video (AI output varies; you may re-generate with a clearer description).
  • Videos generated with incorrect details (shop name, phone number, offer text) entered by the user.
  • Requests made after the 7-day window for unused credits.

7. How to request a refund

Email us at someswararao.pyla@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 73309 84620 with your registered mobile/email and payment reference. We respond within 2 business days.
